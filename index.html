Esoteric sources of the avant-garde

Interactive maps of occultists, Theosophists, spiritualists, abstract painters, visual-music and experimental film-makers, the counterculture, light shows, industrial music and rave, 1840s–2000s.

  • The network — people and groups by period; drag nodes, toggle layers, hover lines for the connection.
  • Proximity through time — who is close to whom, who connects the eras (mavens), and how influence ran forward; flat or rotatable 3D.
  • The timeline — real and planned light, sound and ritual events, with lineage lines.
  • The ideas map — where occult and Theosophical ideas meet AV performance, stage magic and the New Age, with evidence-graded links, themed strands and proof chains you can present step by step.
  • Proof chains — thirteen short chains of people and events who met or worked together, from the séance, the spirit cabinet and the Victorian ghost show to stage magic, the light show, the club, cinema and video art; built for a 1080p screen.
